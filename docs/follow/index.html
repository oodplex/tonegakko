TONEGAKKO 0.40-beta1: the FM-1's screen, drawn here from the game as it plays.

Plug the FM-1 in by USB, then press Connect and allow MIDI with SysEx.

How it works

The FM-1 doesn't send its pixels. Each frame it sends only what changed in the game (where each Sonari stands, who sings, the clock), a few kilobytes a second. This page runs TONEGAKKO's own drawing on that, so the picture is the FM-1's, pixel for pixel, at its frame rate.

Recording

Record saves a WebM video, 720 by 720. Under Sound choose the FM-1 (it's a USB audio input, TONEGAKKO FM-1). Chrome asks first. Keep this tab in front while recording: Chrome pauses pages in the background.

If nothing shows

The FM-1 needs TONEGAKKO 0.21 or later, and Chrome or Edge. Close other pages using the FM-1's MIDI (the installer, a DAW's SysEx). Nothing leaves your computer.
